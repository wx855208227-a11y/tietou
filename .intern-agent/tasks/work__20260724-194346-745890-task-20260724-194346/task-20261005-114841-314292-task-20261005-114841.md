## Review Brief
### Original Request
在不改动当前优质答案内容的前提下，按普心90题、实验25题、测量45题逐题人工检查：先逐字继承速背手册Word中对应表述的加粗与下划线，再按用户既定关键词标准补充非重合内容；每题独立记录来源、继承词、补充词和复核结果，最终生成合订Word/PDF及逐题台账。禁止一次性批量覆盖后以程序自检代替逐题校对。

### Assigned Scope
- Review all 160 existing Shenzhen manual questions sequentially: general psychology 1–90, experimental psychology 1–25, and psychological/educational measurement 1–45.
- Keep the current question selection, answer wording, scoring points, expansions, hierarchy and layout unchanged unless a source character must be restored to preserve an inherited keyword.
- For every question, first inherit the exact bold/underlined character spans from reused speed-review wording; then review only the remaining non-overlapping wording under the established supplemental-keyword standard.
- Treat source consistency as the hard rule. Supplemental marking may not overwrite, widen or contradict a source keyword boundary.

### Deliverables
- Three manually reviewed V18 Markdown/DOCX/PDF subject manuals.
- One combined general—experiment—measurement V18 DOCX/PDF.
- A 160-row manual review ledger recording source inheritance, supplemental keyword decisions, changes and pass status for each individual question.
- A two-way keyword difference report and structural/layout QA report.

### Validation Plan
- Present and inspect one question at a time together with its corresponding speed-review Word evidence; never approve a subject in a single bulk operation.
- Forward check: every speed-review emphasized span reused by the corresponding answer is inherited at the same character boundary.
- Reverse check: every V18 emphasized span is either source-inherited or explicitly recorded as a structural/supplemental scoring keyword.
- Confirm plain text remains identical to the approved V17 content baseline, apart from documented source-character restoration if any.
- Re-render all Word/PDF artifacts and verify counts, font size, page breaks, table/paragraph coverage, directories, blank pages and overflow.

### Constraints And Non-Goals
- Do not use one global matching pass as a substitute for per-question review.
- Do not invent scoring points, alter answer capacity or rewrite approved content while fixing keyword formatting.
- Do not indiscriminately bold every repeated occurrence of a term; inherit formatting only from its corresponding source fragment and judge remaining content by the agreed keyword standard.
- Preserve unrelated files and user edits.
