# 一本通 Tips 呈现形式固定标准

This note records the leader-confirmed Tips format reference for future 一本通 compilation. Use it together with `requirements.md`; when content rules conflict with formatting examples, content rules still come from the textbook, target-school outline, and true questions.

## Confirmed Format Samples

- Primary Tips and supplement format sample: `references/format-samples/shenda-general-psychology-yibentong-format-sample.docx`.
- Secondary generated Tips comparison sample: `references/format-samples/chapter-01-yibentong-v11-selective-tips.docx`.
- The 深大普心 sample was confirmed by the leader as the standardized source for Tips logo, background block, supplementary-content presentation, cover, catalog, fonts, and broader document formatting.
- The v11 sample was confirmed earlier as a good generated Tips direction. Use it as a comparison sample only; when it conflicts with the 深大普心 sample, follow the 深大普心 sample.
- Use these samples for visual form only: block structure, `铁头tips` logo feel, border/fill, spacing, title placement, table/visual-in-Tips presentation, and how examples/supplements/comparison aids sit inside the block.
- Do not use either sample as content authority for level decisions, wording, true-question labels, deletion/compression, or knowledge-point selection.

## Fixed Tips Style Principles

- Tips should appear as a visually independent learning block placed immediately after the relevant knowledge point.
- Tips should not look like ordinary正文 paragraphs. The block should have a clear `铁头tips` identity, light-blue fill/shading, compact inner spacing, and a title when the block has a specific function.
- The `铁头tips` identity should follow the 深大 sample's upper-left logo/textbox style where possible: `铁头` in 方正舒体-like Chinese font and `tips` in Kristen ITC/华文行楷-like font, about 10 pt, bold, transparent/no-fill textbox over the block.
- Tips titles should be short, centered when feasible, and describe the function, such as `易混辨析：...`、`补充内容：...`、`图示：...`、`例如 ...`.
- Summary tables, comparison tables, and diagrams should be placed inside this Tips block style, not as standalone正文 sections.
- Supplementary content follows the 深大 sample's `补充内容：` double-line box style; when it is used as a Tips-type extension, keep the supplement box style rather than forcing a plain Tips body.
- The block must be readable on A4 black-and-white printing: sufficient contrast, readable body font, no dependence on color alone, and no tiny text.
- Keep each Tips block together on one page when feasible. Do not leave the upper-left label separated from the block body, and avoid splitting tables/visuals across pages. If the first-page part of a Tips block would be very short, move the whole Tips block to the next page; if only a short tail spills to the next page, reduce the Tips inner line spacing/spacing slightly only when readability and aesthetics remain acceptable.
- Avoid hard page breaks as the default Tips solution. If a Tips image/table creates a large blank area, first compact the block, redraw the image with a better A4 aspect ratio, reduce nonessential spacing, and rely on keep/cantSplit controls. Only force a page break when the resulting page is still visually balanced.

## Content Types Allowed In Tips

- Textbook experiment or demonstration adaptation, with page marker when available.
- Supplementary content from traceable published textbooks or academic literature, starting with `补充内容：xxxx`.
- Life-close examples for genuinely difficult 一级/二级 points.
- Comparison tables, flowcharts, timelines, or simple visual summaries serving at least one 一级 point.
