# 一本通 Tips 呈现形式固定标准

This note records the leader-confirmed Tips format reference for future 一本通 compilation. Use it together with `requirements.md`; when content rules conflict with formatting examples, content rules still come from the textbook, target-school outline, and true questions.

## Confirmed Format Sample

- Primary Tips format sample: `references/format-samples/chapter-01-yibentong-v11-selective-tips.docx`.
- This sample was confirmed by the leader as a good format direction, especially for Tips presentation.
- Use the sample for Tips visual form only: block structure, label/ribbon feel, border/fill, spacing, title placement, table/visual-in-Tips presentation, and how examples/supplements/comparison aids sit inside the block.
- Do not use the sample as content authority for level decisions, wording, true-question labels, deletion/compression, or knowledge-point selection.

## Fixed Tips Style Principles

- Tips should appear as a visually independent learning block placed immediately after the relevant knowledge point.
- Tips should not look like ordinary正文 paragraphs. The block should have a clear `铁头 tips` identity, visible border/fill, compact inner spacing, and a title when the block has a specific function.
- Tips titles should be short, centered when feasible, and describe the function, such as `易混辨析：...`、`补充内容：...`、`图示：...`、`例如 ...`.
- Summary tables, comparison tables, diagrams, and supplementary content should be placed inside this Tips block style, not as standalone正文 sections.
- The block must be readable on A4 black-and-white printing: sufficient contrast, readable body font, no dependence on color alone, and no tiny text.
- Keep each Tips block together on one page when feasible. Do not leave the upper-left label separated from the block body, and avoid splitting tables/visuals across pages.

## Content Types Allowed In Tips

- Textbook experiment or demonstration adaptation, with page marker when available.
- Supplementary content from traceable published textbooks or academic literature, starting with `补充内容：xxxx`.
- Life-close examples for genuinely difficult 一级/二级 points.
- Comparison tables, flowcharts, timelines, or simple visual summaries serving at least one 一级 point.
